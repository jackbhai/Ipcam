LOCALCAM PRO

Your home. Your network. Your privacy.

A transparent, local-first Android camera and monitor app for homes, nurseries, and private spaces. No accounts. No cloud upload. No hidden recording.

View the project on GitHub →

● Visible by design

Clear camera, microphone, streaming, and recording indicators keep everyone informed.

Local-first

Designed for same-Wi‑Fi and hotspot use, with no mandatory internet or cloud service.

Two roles

Use one Android phone as the camera and another as the monitor.

Secure pairing

Pairing identity and local control are designed to reject unknown devices.

LocalCam Pro is under active development. WebRTC transport and physical-device validation are required before production release.
